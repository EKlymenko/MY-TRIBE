Stage 1 is the first three-month My Tribe experience.

The critical moment

You know how to build, carry responsibility and keep moving. From the outside, things may look convincing. Inside, there may be exhaustion, financial uncertainty, loneliness at the top or too many possible directions.

The old way still delivers some results. It also takes your best attention, leaving the uncertain transition for later. Familiar patterns repeat while a different way remains untested.

Waiting can mean another cycle of exhaustion, more deferred decisions or staying in a role or business model you have already outgrown.

Who it is for

Founders, executives and business owners who carry significant responsibility and are ready to examine how they live and lead.

  • Your results come at too high a personal cost.
  • Your next chapter, role or direction no longer feels obvious.
  • You want honest feedback and are willing to experiment.
  • You can offer others the same attention and reciprocity you seek.

No headhunting. No selling to one another. No comparing funding rounds.

What you leave with after three months

Calm + Clarity + Focus + Action + Evidence + Direction

  • A clearer understanding of your real challenge.
  • One main Transformation Case and your own criteria for success.
  • Awareness of patterns that keep the old situation in place.
  • A clearer view of moments where another choice is possible.
  • New behaviour tested through real-world experiments.
  • External evidence of movement, not only an internal sense of change.
  • Next decisions grounded in experience rather than anxiety, pressure or habit.
  • A more credible next direction.

This is not a promise to completely change your life or business in three months.

Three months. One meaningful transition.

Current programme outline · elements are being tested

Month 1: Calm, clarity and focus

Create space from the internal noise. Identify the real challenge, choose one Transformation Case, define success criteria and describe the movement you want.

Month 2: Patterns, choice and new behaviour

Recognise old behavioural loops. Find moments of choice, develop alternative responses and begin practical experiments. Reflect with the group and your Buddy.

Month 3: Experiments, evidence and next direction

Test new behaviour in real life, collect external evidence and refine decisions. Use feedback from the group and your continuing Buddy to choose a realistic next direction.

How the experience works

Facilitated group work, one private executive session with Jane and regular Buddy conversations support the same real-life case.

Between sessions, you try different responses in your actual work and life. Reflection connects what happened with what you choose to do next.

Why the group

Leadership can leave you surrounded by people and still alone with your real questions.

The group brings perspectives from people carrying comparable responsibility. It offers honest reflection, shared learning and a place where you do not have to perform certainty.

Participants are selected for readiness, reciprocity and willingness to experiment. Confidentiality is a condition of participation.

Why the Buddy

The same Buddy stays with you for all three months, building continuity and trust.

  • Meet for 30 minutes every week.
  • Notice commitments, experiments and evidence.
  • Record important observations between group sessions.
  • At the end, offer evidence-based feedback on the movement you witnessed.

A Buddy is not your therapist, coach, consultant or problem-solver.

Current format

  • Duration: three months / 12 weeks.
  • Group rhythm: one two-hour facilitated group session every week.
  • Individual work: one private executive session with Jane.
  • Buddy: the same Buddy for all three months.
  • Buddy rhythm: 30 minutes every week.
  • Between sessions: experiments, reflection and evidence collection.
  • Delivery: online.
  • Entry: application and fit conversation.
  • Selection: comparable responsibility, readiness for change, reciprocity and willingness to experiment.
  • Group size, language and dates: To be validated.

Who it is not for

This is not a ready-made formula for earning more in three months. It is not a networking club or a place to sell to others.

It asks for honest reflection and practical participation. If you only want advice without examining your own part in the situation, this will not be the right format.
